WELCOME TO DESTINATION CHURCH

A Family Called to Purpose

Destination Church is a vibrant, Spirit-filled community where lives are transformed through the power of God's Word. We believe every person has a God-given purpose and a destiny to fulfill — and we exist to help you discover, walk in and fulfill it.

Under the leadership of Prophet David Kojo Kyei, our church is committed to raising a people who know their identity in Christ, live with purpose, and pursue their divine destiny with boldness and faith.

— Prophet David Kojo Kyei, Lead Pastor
